Privacy Policy

Coarse Correct — Last updated April 8, 2026

The short version

Coarse Correct does not collect, store, or share any of your personal data. Everything stays on your device.

What data the app uses

The app stores your brew journal entries and preferences using your device's local storage. This data:

  • Lives only on your device
  • Is never sent to any server
  • Is never shared with anyone
  • Is deleted if you clear the app's data or uninstall the app

What we do not collect

We do not collect:

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or tracking data
  • Device identifiers
  • Cookies or advertising data

No accounts

The app does not require or offer user accounts. There is no sign-up, login, or registration of any kind.

No third-party services

The app does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's privacy

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
